OpenRadio Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

OpenRadio is an internet radio player for Android. This policy explains what information the app uses and where it goes. In short: OpenRadio has no accounts, no ads and no analytics, and the developer does not collect or store any of your personal data.

Information stored on your device

Your favourites, recently played stations, settings and your chosen region are saved only on your device. They are never uploaded to us. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes them.

Location (optional)

OpenRadio can suggest stations near you. This is off until you turn it on, and Android asks for your permission first. Only approximate location is needed, and you can choose "Approximate" in the Android permission dialog.

  • The app rounds your position to about 10 km as soon as it is read.
  • That rounded position is sent to the Radio Browser station directory (radio-browser.info) to search for nearby stations.
  • Inside India, your state is worked out on your device from a built-in list of towns.
  • The app saves only the resulting region (and the rounded position) on your device. Location is not tracked in the background.

You can deny or revoke the permission at any time in Android Settings and pick your region by hand instead.

Third-party services the app connects to

  • Radio Browser (radio-browser.info), a free community station directory. The app sends your searches, the rounded location described above when you use "near me", and a play count when you start a station (this is how station popularity is calculated). It does not send any identifier for you or your device.
  • Radio stations' own servers. When you play a station, audio and station logos are loaded directly from that broadcaster's servers.

Like any internet connection, these services can see your device's IP address and basic request details. Their own privacy policies apply to that information.

Information we do not collect

OpenRadio does not collect your name, email, contacts, files, microphone, camera, advertising ID or precise location. It contains no advertising or analytics SDKs. Personal data is not sold or shared for advertising.

Children

OpenRadio is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.
